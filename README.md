# Tutorials Repository

This repository stores tutorials created by Claude on various subjects.

## Organization Guidelines

### File Structure
- Each tutorial is saved as a **markdown (.md) file** with a **meaningful, descriptive name**
- Tutorial subjects can cover any topic
- Related files (tutorials on similar topics or with dependencies) are grouped in **folders with meaningful names**

### Naming Conventions
- Use clear, descriptive names that reflect the tutorial content
- Examples:
  - `python-basics.md`
  - `react-hooks-guide.md`
  - `docker-compose-tutorial.md`

### Folder Organization
- Group related tutorials together
- Examples:
  ```
  python/
    ├── python-basics.md
    ├── advanced-decorators.md
    └── async-programming.md

  web-development/
    ├── react-hooks-guide.md
    ├── css-flexbox.md
    └── responsive-design.md
  ```

## Current Tutorials

### AI & Machine Learning (`ai-ml/`)
- **[How LLMs Work Internally](ai-ml/how-llms-work-internally.md)** - Comprehensive guide explaining the internal workings of Large Language Models, including tokenization, embeddings, transformer architecture, attention mechanisms, training, and inference. Designed for software engineers without ML background.
- **[The AI Takeoff and Future](ai-ml/ai-takeoff-and-future.md)** - Clear breakdown of AI's accelerating progress and path to superintelligence. Covers current state (2025), near-term predictions (2025-2027), the 2030s vision of abundant intelligence and energy, self-reinforcing loops (recursive improvement, automation), and social/economic implications. Explains how wonders become routine and what it means for humanity's future.
- **[Google Research 2025: The Magic Cycle](ai-ml/google-research-2025-magic-cycle.md)** - Concise overview of Google Research's 2025 breakthroughs and the "Magic Cycle" accelerating research-to-product translation. Covers advances in generative AI (factuality, efficiency, generative UI), quantum computing breakthrough (Quantum Echoes 13,000x faster), AI-powered scientific discovery (AI co-scientist), domain advances (genomics, neuroscience), Earth AI and climate resilience (FireSat), health AI (AMIE), education (LearnLM), and novel ML architectures. Explains how AI is becoming the tool that accelerates all research.

### History (`history/`)
- **[French History Essentials](history/french-history-essentials.md)** - Thematic journey through French history from medieval times to present day. Focuses on key concepts, movements, and cultural impact rather than overwhelming dates. Covers the Revolution, Napoleon, Belle Époque, World Wars, and modern France's identity. Perfect for intellectuals seeking essential knowledge about French civilization.
- **[American History Essentials](history/american-history-essentials.md)** - Narrative exploration of United States history focusing on ideas, contradictions, and cultural impact. Covers colonial origins, Revolution, Constitution, expansion, Civil War, Gilded Age, World Wars, Cold War, Civil Rights, and modern America. Emphasizes key concepts like exceptionalism, individualism, race, and the ongoing American experiment.

### Space & Astronomy (`space/`)
- **[Spacecraft Navigation](space/spacecraft-navigation.md)** - Comprehensive guide to how spacecraft navigate to distant destinations in the constantly moving solar system. Covers orbital mechanics, trajectory planning, launch windows, navigation techniques, course corrections, gravity assists, and real mission examples. Explains the challenges of vast distances, communication delays, and precision requirements. Accessible to non-specialists while being detailed and self-contained.

### Professional Development (`professional-development/`)
- **[The Coaching Habit Guide](professional-development/coaching-habit-guide.md)** - Practical coaching framework based on Michael Bungay Stanier's "The Coaching Habit." Master the seven essential questions that transform leadership: kickstart question, AWE question ("And what else?"), focus question, foundation question, lazy question, strategic question, and learning question. Learn to tame the Advice Monster, stay curious longer, and build team independence. Includes real scenarios, common pitfalls, daily practice guide, and quick reference.
- **[Coaching Conversation Example](professional-development/coaching-conversation-example.md)** - Complete 15-minute end-to-end coaching conversation demonstrating all seven questions from The Coaching Habit in action. Shows coach and client dialogue covering product launch overwhelm, reveals how questions uncover deeper patterns (control, defining value), includes moment-by-moment analysis, comparison with traditional advice-giving approach, and practice exercises. Perfect companion to the main guide showing the questions working together in real context.

### Personal Development (`personal-development/`)
- **[Quit Smoking: Mindset Transformation Guide](personal-development/quit-smoking-mindset-guide.md)** - Practical daily strategies for changing mindset to quit smoking through identity transformation rather than willpower. Covers the shift from "smoker trying to quit" to "non-smoker," daily mindset practices (morning/evening rituals), reframing triggers and cravings (RAIN technique), 30-day mindset map, tools for difficult moments (10-minute rule, urge surfing), building new rituals, and dealing with setbacks with self-compassion. Includes evidence gathering, identity affirmations, replacement behaviors, and comprehensive support strategies for sustainable change.
